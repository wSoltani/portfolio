## Concept

Speech analytics for call center quality. Call recordings are scored by AI against compliance criteria, including required legal statements, and the results are turned into statistics.

## My part

This was a joint project with AlmavivA's data scientists, built as microservices, and it shipped. I worked full stack but mostly on the frontend, where I built the statistics and data handling, and an audio player with a synced transcript that works like a lyrics player: words highlight as they're spoken, and clicking a word jumps to that point in the recording. On the backend, I wrote Java packages used across the microservices.

## Stack

- Frontend: Angular
- Backend: Spring Boot microservices (Java)
- Database: MongoDB
